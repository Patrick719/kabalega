Welcome to KASSOBA

The Kabalega Old Students Association is the official body for all alumni of Kabalega Secondary School. We are a community dedicated to fostering lifelong connections, supporting our alma mater, and celebrating our shared legacy.

Why Join KABOSA?

Network & Connect

Reconnect with old classmates and build professional relationships with a vast network of successful alumni.

Exclusive Events

Get access to reunions, homecoming events, workshops, and social gatherings held throughout the year.

Give Back

Contribute to the development of Kabalega SS through mentorship programs, scholarships, and fundraising initiatives.

Celebrate Success

Stay updated on the achievements of fellow alumni and celebrate the collective success of the Kabalegan family.
